Proposal automation pipelineIn development. n8n, Claude, Qdrant, MCP

Problem
A proposal normally takes 5 to 7 days by hand, and most of that time goes on formatting and boilerplate.
What I built
An agentic workflow from RFP intake to a submission-ready draft. It masks client PII before anything reaches the model, drafts from the client brief and company credentials, validates against a compliance rulebook, then stops for human approval.
A decision
Client details are masked before anything reaches the model, not cleaned up afterwards, so sensitive data can’t leak through a bad draft. I also kept a human approval step at the end, so reviewers spend their time on strategy, pricing and client relationships instead of formatting.
Result so far
Still in development, so I’m not claiming a final time saving yet. The masking, retrieval, compliance check and approval stages are built, and the goal is to bring the 5 to 7 day cycle down.
Next
Run it on more past proposals and measure the real time saved before putting a number on it.

RAG for internal documentsOngoing. Python, LangGraph, Qdrant

Problem
Bids and reports were hard to search, and keyword search missed domain-specific terms.
What I built
A retrieval system with hybrid keyword and semantic search, plus a relevance scoring step that re-ranks passages before they reach the model. I also tuned chunking, indexing and query routing.
A decision
Keyword search alone kept missing industry-specific terms, so I added semantic vector search on top of it. Then I added a scoring step that re-ranks the passages, so the most relevant ones reach the model first.
Result
Manual document search effort down by 60%. My estimate, based on how long the team spent searching for bid documents before and after.
